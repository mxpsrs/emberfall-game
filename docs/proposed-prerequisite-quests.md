# Three main-story quests — authorized ordered chain

Raymond reviewed this sequence, required linear main-story progression, and authorized implementation. He corrected the third quest to use a cursed shrine, enchanted oathstones, stolen voices and a memory shard.

## 1. The Broken Watch

- Start: Captain Rellan in Briarhaven. Recommended Combat 8; no skill gate.
- Story: A courier vanished while carrying orders that would withdraw the road patrols. Rellan suspects a raid, but the attack was arranged to conceal a forged order.
- Play: interview the surviving wagon driver; inspect the abandoned cart and two contradictory clues; follow the correct trail; defeat the raider lookout; recover the dispatch; expose the forged seal to Rellan.
- Choices: an incorrect suspect or trail produces a useful contradiction and lets the player investigate again; no permanent failure.
- Reward: 150 coins, 200 Attack XP and 200 Defense XP. The forged dispatch becomes permanent journal evidence.
- Purpose: establishes forged royal orders and the danger on the road to Ironhollow.

## 2. The Weight of an Oath

- Start: Forewoman Hesta at Ironhollow's surface mine. Requires The Broken Watch, Mining 5 and Smithing 5.
- Story: A broken freight lift has trapped two workers. Their supervisor was ordered to abandon them. Hesta asks the player to help bring them home.
- Play: inspect the jammed lift; open a safe ventilation route; recover usable metal from a collapsed support; forge a replacement locking pin; set the lift's brake and counterweight in the correct order; escort both workers to the surface; report to Hesta.
- Choices: an unsafe mechanism order stops the lift and explains the problem without killing a worker or resetting the quest.
- Reward: 200 coins, 250 Mining XP and 250 Smithing XP. Hesta's signed testimony stays in the journal.
- Purpose: establishes the ward-keepers' duty to protect people and earns the miners' trust.

## 3. Echoes Without a Name

- Start: Scholar Ilyra at a roadside shrine near Ironhollow. Requires The Weight of an Oath and Magic 5; recommended Combat 12.
- Story: An abandoned shrine repeats voices belonging to people who are still alive. Ilyra needs its source isolated before more travelers follow it underground.
- Play: investigate three enchanted oathstones; compare their stolen memories against witness statements; solve a three-symbol ward sequence using the evidence; confront an echo shade; break the enchantment; bring Ilyra its whispering memory shard.
- Choices: wrong answers provide clues and can be retried. Losing the fight preserves evidence and puzzle progress.
- Reward: 250 coins, 300 Magic XP and 150 Hitpoints XP. The memory shard stays in the journal.
- Purpose: introduces the borrowed-voice mystery without revealing the mountain story's main twist.

## Integration

- Raymond requires a linear main-story chain: The Broken Watch → The Weight of an Oath → Echoes Without a Name → The King Beneath the Mountain. Each completed quest unlocks the next. Captain Rellan sends the player to Hesta with the forged order; the rescued workers identify the cursed shrine and send the player to Ilyra; Ilyra sends the memory shard and testimony to Maerin.
- Characters already partway through the mountain story retain access and progress; the new quests remain playable for their one-time rewards.
- Each stage has journal history, one clear current objective and a working location guide.
- Evidence and bound quest tools are saved with quest state, never dropped or lost through a full inventory, death or logout.
- Rewards are granted once, with recovery from an interrupted turn-in and no duplicate reward exploits.
- Validate real interactions, reachable locations, wrong answers, death/retry, reconnect, reward delivery and the final prerequisite gate before release.
